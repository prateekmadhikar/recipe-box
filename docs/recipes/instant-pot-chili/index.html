04

Make it your own

Easy swaps & a few useful notes.

No fire-roasted tomatoes?

Use the same amount of regular canned diced tomatoes. The extra ½ tsp smoked paprika for the whole recipe adds smoke; ¼ tsp chipotle powder adds optional heat. Both are already accounted for in the prep instructions.

Use the beans you find

Replace any of the four cans with the same size cans of black, navy/white haricot, cannellini, or Romano/cranberry beans. Drain and rinse them. Canned Romano beans are a good fit; fresh flat Romano green beans are a different ingredient. Use cooked canned beans here, not dried beans.

A note on the original recipe

Adapted from your shared chili conversation. The flavour proportions are preserved, with 3 cups broth instead of 2 so each of the two 6-quart batches has 1½ cups thin liquid. Pressure release is fully natural. This is an untested adaptation; follow the liquid minimum and fill limits in your own cooker’s manual. Larger cans change volume, so recheck the fill line.

05

Save some for later

A little planning. More good dinners.

Cool & store

Divide into shallow containers and refrigerate or freeze within 2 hours. Keep only the next 2–3 days’ meals in the fridge at 4°C or colder and freeze the rest. Label containers with the date. Do not keep the whole week’s chili in the fridge.

Reheat a portion

Thaw in the fridge or microwave. Reheat to 74°C / 165°F throughout, stirring so the centre heats evenly. Add a splash of water or broth if needed; only reheat the portion you plan to eat.
